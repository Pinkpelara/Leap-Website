- [July SEO notes](research/seo-2026-07/): historical observations and proposed fixes. Do not treat old diagnoses or instructions as current authority.

The 39 original files are preserved byte-for-byte. `manifest.json` records their SHA-256 hashes and source-relative names. No originals were moved or deleted. The application, current-state documentation and October website capture remain the starting point for ongoing work.

This is not a complete backup of every Leap folder. Private correspondence, child/customer records, signed forms, corporate paperwork, browser profiles, private access links, credentials and unreviewed photos/videos are not included. Changing repository visibility later cannot retract public copies.
