# The work that actually brings parents in

This is the highest-return part of the whole project, and almost none of it is website
work. Ranked by impact.

---

## First: the honest verdict on Soro and Jumper Media

**Do not buy either. One is wasted money; the other can get your Google listing removed.**

### Jumper Media "local rank" — actively dangerous

Their product is built on **fake engagement**: simulated GPS/location activity designed to
manufacture local ranking signals. It's sold in softer words ("GPS-based local activity",
"motivates local engagement"), but that's what it is.

Google's fake-engagement policy prohibits exactly this. The penalty is **suspension of the
Business Profile**.

For you that is the worst possible outcome: your listing *is* your enrolment channel. Losing
it to save a few months of legitimate work would be a catastrophic trade. This isn't a
grey area — avoid it.

### Soro — not dangerous, just pointed at the wrong target

Soro auto-writes and auto-publishes daily AI blog posts. Two problems:

1. It matches Google's named example of **"scaled content abuse"** — generating many pages
   primarily to influence rankings. Since March 2024 the quality assessment is **site-wide**,
   so a pile of generic posts can drag down the good pages you already have.
2. Even if it worked perfectly, it targets the wrong searches. Nobody enrols a child because
   they read "10 Benefits of Gymnastics for Kids". Your parents come from *"gymnastics near
   me"* — a map result, which no blog post can influence.

It also can't publish to Uplifter (no integration), so it isn't even mechanically possible.

### So what does "better than Soro and Jumper" mean?

Not out-publishing them and not out-gaming them. **Winning the map pack legitimately**, which
is what both are badly imitating. That's the plan below.

## 1. Google Business Profile — the single biggest lever

Independent 2026 local-ranking analysis puts **profile signals at roughly a third** of what
decides the map pack, and **review signals at about a fifth**. Together that's over half —
versus effectively zero for blog volume.

### Fields to set

```
Name:               Leap Gymnastics
                    (do NOT keyword-stuff it to "Leap Gymnastics Markham Kids Classes" —
                     that violates Google's naming policy and risks suspension)
Primary category:   Gymnastics center
Secondary:          Children's party service
                    Summer camp
                    Gymnastics club
Address:            158 Anderson Ave, Units 3 & 4, Markham, ON
Service areas:      Markham · Whitchurch-Stouffville · Unionville · Richmond Hill
Hours:              Monday–Sunday, real opening hours (currently missing — this is a
                    documented ranking factor, not just a convenience)
Opening date:       month + year the club opened
Booking link:       your registration page on www.leapgymnastics.ca
```

### The description (750 characters max)

Google does **not** use the description text for ranking — it's conversion copy. Its job is
to make a parent choose you once you already appear. Write it for them, not for Google.

> Leap Gymnastics is a Gymnastics Ontario member club in Markham, offering gymnastics
> classes for kids from 18 months to teens — parent-and-tot, preschool, recreational classes
> for girls and boys, tumbling, and a competitive team. We also run PA Day, March Break and
> summer camps, gymnastics birthday parties, and coached trial sessions for families new to
> the gym. Serving Markham, Stouffville, Unionville and York Region, in our own dedicated
> gymnastics facility with NCCP-trained coaches.

Rules Google enforces here: no URLs, no phone numbers, no promotional/sales language, no
special characters. The text above complies.

**Only the first ~250 characters show before "More"** — so the club type, the town and the
age range all sit up front deliberately.

### Also worth doing

- **Photos**: exterior (so people recognise the building), entrance, gym floor, equipment,
  a class in progress, coaches. Refresh periodically — photo activity is an engagement signal.
- **Products/Services**: one entry per program, named the way parents search (see file 3).
- **Q&A**: seed the real questions you get asked — what age can they start, what should
  they wear, do you offer a trial, is there parent seating. You're allowed to ask and answer
  your own.

## 2. Reviews — the most under-used lever you have

Last known state: **5.0 stars, ~8 reviews** (worth re-checking). The rating is excellent;
the *count* is the problem. Review count and **recency** are among the strongest signals you
can actually move — a steady trickle beats a burst.

**Your review link:** `https://g.page/r/CQRc3-oyAt1TEBM/review`
*(Sourced from an earlier report — verify it opens the review box before using it widely.)*

### Rules that matter

- **Never incentivise.** No discounts, no free classes, no draw entries for a review. Google
  prohibits it and will strip reviews (or worse).
- **Never gate.** Don't ask "did you have a good experience?" and only route the happy ones
  to Google. That's review gating and it's against policy.
- Ask **everyone**, plainly, at a natural moment.

### The natural moments

1. End of a **trial session** — the parent has just watched their child have fun. Highest
   conversion moment you have.
2. End of a **camp week**.
3. End of a **session/term**, in the wrap-up email.
4. After a **birthday party** — parents are often delighted and never asked.

### Wording that complies and works

> Thanks for a great session with us! If your family enjoyed it, a short Google review
> genuinely helps other Markham and Stouffville parents find us. It takes about a minute:
> [link]

**Why this beats Jumper Media outright:** they sell fake signals at the risk of your listing.
Ten real reviews a term is a stronger, permanent, un-penalisable version of the same thing.

## 3. Directory listings — where these searches actually land

Research finding: for "gymnastics classes Markham" type searches, **directory sites outrank
club websites**, and your competitors are listed while you're thin. This is a distribution
gap no amount of website editing fixes.

Get listed on (most have free tiers):

| Directory | Why |
|---|---|
| Help! We've Got Kids | Strong GTA parent traffic; list **both** Markham *and* Whitchurch-Stouffville |
| ChatterBlock | Ranks well for Markham kids' activities |
| ourkids.net | Dominates camp searches, esp. PA Day camps |
| camps.ca | Seasonal camp searches |
| KidsPrograms.ca | Competitors already listed here |
| Child's Life | GTA parent audience |
| LivelyVillage | Local activity listings |
| Yelp Canada | Feeds other surfaces |

Use identical name, address and phone everywhere — consistency is itself a ranking signal.

Also: make sure your **Gymnastics Ontario "Find a Club"** listing links to
`https://www.leapgymnastics.ca` (not the uplifterinc address). That's a high-authority,
perfectly on-topic link and it's free.

## 4. The Stouffville opening — the cheapest win available

The research found **no dedicated artistic gymnastics club ranking in Stouffville results**.
The local operators are fitness/tumbling hybrids. You already serve Stouffville families,
but your site barely says so.

Competition for "gymnastics Stouffville" and "gymnastics classes Stouffville" is **low** —
these are the easiest rankings on your entire keyword list.

This needs one short paragraph added (not a rewrite) — see file 3.
