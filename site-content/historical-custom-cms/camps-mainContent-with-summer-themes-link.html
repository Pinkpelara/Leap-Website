Camps

LEAP camp days give children a full day of movement, creativity, and supervised activity in a gymnastics setting.

Campers spend time on the gym floor with coached gymnastics activities, games, obstacle courses, crafts, and active play. Each day includes a balance of structured movement, group activities, creative time, and lunch and snack breaks, so children stay active while still having time to rest and reset.

Camp programming is designed for children ages 4 to 12. Activities are planned with age, readiness, and safety in mind, and campers are grouped and supervised by coaches throughout the day.

LEAP offers camp days during school breaks and select school closure days, including Summer Camp, PA Day Camp, and March Break Camp when available.

Campers must be fully potty-trained and able to participate independently in a group setting. Camp is best suited for children who are comfortable following coach instructions, staying with their group, and taking part in a full day of activities without one-on-one support.

Camp groups use a 1:8 coach-to-participant ratio unless otherwise stated.

Camp pricing starts at $72 per day and $272 per week.

Families can view current camp dates, availability, full-week and individual-day options, and any available add-ons on the registration page.

All programs are subject to the applicable Gymnastics Ontario fee.

Summer Gymnastics Classes

LEAP summer classes are weekly gymnastics programs for children who want to stay active, continue building skills, and keep moving during the summer season.

Summer classes are separate from camp. They are a good option for families who want a consistent weekly gymnastics class rather than a full-day camp program.

Each class includes coached gymnastics instruction, age-appropriate progressions, and skill development on the equipment. Gymnasts work on strength, flexibility, balance, coordination, confidence, and foundational gymnastics skills in a structured class setting.

Classes are grouped by age and program, so participants can work with gymnasts at a similar stage of development.

Summer classes use a 1:8 coach-to-participant ratio unless otherwise stated.

Summer class pricing starts at $200.

Families can view current class dates, times, availability, and registration options on the summer class calendar.

All programs are subject to the applicable Gymnastics Ontario fee.

What Camps Include

Leap camps mix coached gymnastics with games, crafts, and supervised group activities, plus lunch and snacks.

01

Gymnastics Stations

Rolling, jumping, balancing, climbing, swinging, landing, and body-control activities.

02

Obstacle Courses

Climbing, crawling, and tumbling challenges that build strength, coordination, and confidence.

03

Group Activities

Supervised games and group activities between gym rotations.

04

Breaks

Snack, lunch, water, creative activities, and quieter breaks between active gym time.

Good to Know

Camp Requirements
  • Ages 4 to 12
  • Fully potty-trained
  • Comfortable participating independently
  • Able to spend the camp day without a parent or guardian
What to Bring
  • Labelled water bottle
  • Lunch
  • Two snacks
  • Comfortable athletic clothing
  • Hair tied back
  • No jewelry

Campers are barefoot in the gym.

Camp Care

Before care and after care may be available for Summer Camp dates. Current times and prices appear inside the relevant Summer Camp registration details. Camp care is an add-on and is not a separate program.

Registration and Fees

Camps start at $72 per day; Summer Camp weeks are listed at $272 for short holiday weeks and $340 for five-day weeks. Summer classes show their class count and price before registration. Applicable Gymnastics Ontario fees, if required, will appear during checkout.
