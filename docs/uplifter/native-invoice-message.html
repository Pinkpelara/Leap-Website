Birthday party bookings

If you booked a birthday party, your date and selected package are listed in this invoice. Please use the links below to complete your party details and get your guest consent link.

Complete your party form
Get your guest consent link

Use the email address you entered at checkout. We’ll send you a one-time verification code so you can securely open your forms.

Please complete your party form at least seven days before your party, or as soon as possible if your party is sooner. You can save your progress and return later. Select “Save and submit” when you’re ready to send your details to Leap. If you make changes afterwards, select “Save and submit” again so we receive your updates.

Each participating child needs a consent form completed by their own parent or legal guardian before joining the gymnastics activities. Complete one for your child and share your guest consent link with the other families.

Keep this invoice so you can return to your forms and check which guest consent forms we’ve received. If you need help, reply to this email or contact info@leapgymnastics.ca.
