# Leap do's and don'ts

Reconstructed October 23, 2026 from all 21 available Tencent project-memory records, retained working rules, current source and the latest accessible Leap chats. This is not a claim to have recovered every past conversation. Personal information previously removed from memory has not been restored.

## Scope and protection

- Make only requested changes. Authentication grants access, not permission for unrelated edits.
- Use Custom CMS page/shared content. Do not edit Uplifter templates.
- Preserve Beam exactly, including the original loader, assets, styling, position and interactions. Verify it after restoring any old page.
- Treat registered or uncertain programs/products/classes as immutable. Check the exact target's current registration state before any permitted change.
- Preserve older birthday terms, saved choices, private-link routing, legal wording and signed consent history.
- Never change Uplifter checkout, payment, acceptance, cancellation or availability through the custom form system. An intake/form/email failure must not reject an Uplifter booking.
- Do not resend historical communications or replay production jobs without explicit authorization.

## Website quality

- Keep program descriptions, registration cards, calendars, popups, links and FAQ consistent. Check actual destinations and fragments, not only HTTP 200.
- Preserve approved wording, photos and brand colours. Scope CSS and handlers to the feature. Registration styling must remain restrained.
- Test desktop/mobile interaction, links, forms and console errors; audit related pages after changes. Measure performance rather than promising no regression.
- Available-space badges are only Full, 1 spot left, or 2 spots left, from native availability matched by exact SKU. Do not calculate them by subtracting registrations from capacity. Hide uncertain/stale counts and date-specific drop-in counts.
- Friday 4–5 Parent & Tot drop-in was discontinued October 13. Do not restore it from a snapshot. Monday 4–5 Rising Stars was added October 21; do not duplicate it.

## Operational planning

- Expansion prioritizes likely new paid enrolment and exact day/time demand, not theoretical revenue. Separate transfers, additional classes and new children.
- Floor planning limit from owner: 40 people including accompanying parents. Count Parent & Tot child plus adult as two. Do not invent a staff reserve. Parent & Tot must not overlap ages 6–7 or older. Weekend regular classes finish by 1 p.m.
- Full-program fees, monthly instalments and monthly scheduled-session charges are different. Reverify current Uplifter configuration and holidays before making claims; September billing observations are dated.

## Communication and evidence

- Use clear, natural Canadian English and “enrolment.” Avoid padding, invented FAQs and unsupported promises.
- Read the relevant Uplifter KB first. Distinguish official documentation, owner decisions, observed implementation and assistant proposals.
- Prefer newer confirmed decisions. Historical rollback instructions and superseded designs are not permission to redeploy them.
- Keep source-backed memory small. Do not store credentials, personal profiles, raw chats or private customer records.
