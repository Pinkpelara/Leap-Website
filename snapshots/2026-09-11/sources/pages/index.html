About Leap Gymnastics

Leap Gymnastics is a dedicated gymnastics club in Markham, offering programs for athletes at different ages, abilities, and stages of development.

Located in Markham, Leap Gymnastics welcomes families from Markham, Stouffville, and nearby York Region communities.

Our coaching approach focuses on safe skill development, proper technique, age-appropriate instruction, and athlete readiness. Gymnasts build strength, flexibility, coordination, balance, body control, spatial awareness, focus, and confidence through structured gymnastics training led by NCCP-trained coaches.

For school-age recreational gymnasts, Leap follows the CANGYM skill-development pathway where applicable. CANGYM supports progress through recognized skill levels and helps gymnasts, coaches, and families track development over time.

Gymnastics is one of the strongest foundations for physical literacy. The skills athletes develop in gymnastics support movement, confidence, and performance in the gym, in other sports, and in everyday activity.

Our Programs

Recreational Gymnastics
Leap offers progressive weekly gymnastics classes for parent-and-tot, preschool and school-age gymnasts, including boys-focused and floor and tumbling programs. Coaches adapt instruction to each gymnast’s age, ability and readiness.

Classes run from September to June, and children may join an available class at any point during the year. Enrolment is month-to-month, with no full-year commitment.

Advanced Recreational Programs
Leap Plus classes are longer-format recreational programs for gymnasts who are ready for more training time, more repetitions, stronger conditioning, and a faster class pace. These programs are designed for athletes who want to continue progressing in a structured recreational environment.

Competitive Gymnastics
Leap offers a competitive gymnastics pathway for athletes who are ready for a more focused training environment. Entry into the competitive program is by coach assessment. Placement is based on ability, readiness, commitment, training goals, and appropriate program fit.

Camps and Summer Programs
Leap offers P.A. Day camps, March Break camps, summer camps and weekly summer gymnastics classes.

Birthday Parties
Leap offers weekend gymnastics birthday parties in a dedicated gymnastics facility. Birthday parties are separate from Leap’s gymnastics programs and are designed as supervised in-gym celebrations led by Leap staff.

Why Choose Leap

Gymnastics Ontario Member Club
Leap is a Gymnastics Ontario member club and operates within Ontario’s provincial gymnastics system.
 
NCCP-Trained Coaches
Leap’s coaches are trained through the National Coaching Certification Program and provide instruction based on safe progressions, proper technique, and athlete readiness.
 
CANGYM Skill Development
School-age recreational gymnasts follow the CANGYM skill-development pathway where applicable, supporting structured progress and skill recognition.
 
Structured Coaching
Classes are built around proper progressions, safe technique, repetition, correction, and athlete readiness.
 
Small Class Ratios
Classes are capped at 8 gymnasts per coach, allowing for more individual instruction, supervision, and feedback.
 
Dedicated Gymnastics Facility
Gymnasts train in a dedicated facility with gymnastics apparatus and training equipment appropriate to their age, level, and program.
 
Safe Sport Environment
Leap is committed to a safe, respectful, and professional sport environment for gymnasts, coaches, and families.
