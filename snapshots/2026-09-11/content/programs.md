Leap offers coached gymnastics classes for children across Markham and Stouffville, from parent-and-tot and preschool programs to recreational, advanced recreational and competitive pathways.

Recreational Programs
Leap’s recreational programs provide structured weekly gymnastics instruction for children at every stage, from their first parent-and-tot class through advanced recreational training.
As a Gymnastics Ontario member club, Leap uses age-, ability- and readiness-appropriate progressions to support safe and consistent skill development. Depending on the program, gymnasts develop skills across vault, bars, beam and floor while building strength, flexibility, balance, coordination, spatial awareness and confidence.
Children progress from their current ability level rather than following a curriculum that begins each September. This allows new gymnasts to join during the year while existing gymnasts continue working toward increasingly challenging skills.
Recreational classes operate from September to June, and children may join in any month where space is available. Enrolment is month-to-month, with no requirement to commit to the full school year.
Once registered, your child remains in the same weekly class and continues to be enrolled until you choose to withdraw. Families do not need to register again each month.
Withdrawal requires 15 days’ written notice. Please review our
Cancellation and Refund Policy
for complete billing, withdrawal and refund details.

Gymnastics Ontario Participant Registration
Leap Gymnastics is a member club of Gymnastics Ontario. Participants in the September-to-June recreational classes listed on this page are registered with Gymnastics Ontario in accordance with its participant registration requirements.
A
$50 annual participant registration fee
is charged separately from program tuition and is added at checkout when applicable. It is charged once per participant during the Gymnastics Ontario membership year, which runs from July 1 to June 30, regardless of how many applicable Leap programs the participant registers for during that membership year.
The registration includes applicable sport accident insurance coverage while participating in Gymnastics Ontario-sanctioned activities. Once processed, the registration fee is non-refundable.

Little Leapers (Age 3)
Age 3 1 hour
An independent preschool gymnastics program designed specifically for three-year-olds who are ready to participate in class without a parent or caregiver.
Gymnasts are introduced to fundamental gymnastics positions and skills through structured, coach-led stations. Training develops jumping and landing mechanics, rolls, balances, hangs and supports, basic body shapes, coordination and spatial awareness using age-appropriate gymnastics equipment.
The focus is on building strong early gymnastics foundations while developing the listening, independence and body control needed to progress into more advanced preschool gymnastics.

Recreational Classes
Floor Tumbling & Obstacle Skills
Recommended 7+ | 1 hour
A floor-focused recreational gymnastics program for athletes who want more time to develop their gymnastics skills on the floor.
Gymnasts work through structured progressions that develop the foundations needed for strong floor skills, including body positions and control, handstands, rolls, cartwheels, jumping and landing technique, strength, flexibility and coordination. As athletes build the necessary technique and physical readiness, they progress toward increasingly challenging floor and tumbling skills.
Unlike our regular recreational gymnastics programs, this class does not rotate through vault, bars and beam. Training time is dedicated to floor skill development, with obstacle stations and conditioning incorporated to develop the strength, power, agility, spatial awareness and movement control that support gymnastics progression.
No previous gymnastics experience is required. Athletes work at their own level and progress according to their ability and readiness.

Advanced Recreational Programs
Leap Plus is Leap’s advanced recreational pathway for gymnasts who are ready for longer training sessions and more time for repetitions, conditioning, skill development and progression. These structured 90-minute weekly classes provide additional training time while remaining within Leap’s recreational program.
Leap Plus classes operate weekly from September to June. Eligible gymnasts may join during the year where an appropriate class and space are available. Enrolment is month-to-month, with no full-year commitment.
