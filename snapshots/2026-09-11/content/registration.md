Recreational Gymnastics
Leap’s recreational classes provide structured weekly gymnastics instruction from September to June. Coaches adapt progressions to each gymnast’s age, current ability and readiness, allowing children to join during the year where space is available.
Enrolment is month-to-month, with no full-year commitment. Once registered, your child remains in the selected weekly class and is billed monthly until you choose to withdraw. Tuition is $95 per month for one-hour recreational classes and $142.50 per month for Leap Plus.
Families may withdraw with 15 days’ written notice. Please review our Cancellation and Refund Policy for complete billing, withdrawal and refund information.

Recreational Programs
Floor Tumbling & Obstacle Skills
Classes Operate September 2026–June 2027
A floor-focused recreational gymnastics program for athletes who want more time to develop their gymnastics skills on the floor.
Gymnasts work through structured progressions in body control, handstands, rolls, cartwheels, jumping and landing technique, strength, flexibility and coordination, progressing toward increasingly challenging floor and tumbling skills as they are ready.
Unlike our regular recreational gymnastics programs, this class does not rotate through vault, bars and beam. Training is focused on floor skill development, with conditioning and obstacle-based stations used to support strength, power, agility and safe gymnastics progression.
Recommended for ages 7+. No previous gymnastics experience required.
