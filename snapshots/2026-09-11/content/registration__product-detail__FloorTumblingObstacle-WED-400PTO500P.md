Description:
Recommended 7+. 1 hour. A high-energy recreational class that builds strength, balance, coordination, confidence, and beginner floor tumbling skills through rolls, cartwheels, handstands, jumps, safe landings, obstacle activities, and gymnastics-based movement challenges.
Progressive Weekly Gymnastics
This class is part of Leap’s structured School Year recreational program and is designed for consistent instruction and skill progression. Children are welcome to join during the school year where space is available and begin from their current level. Once registered, your child keeps this weekly class spot while enrolled and continues progressing with their group.
Flexible Enrolment
Tuition is $95/month while enrolled. Families may withdraw at any time subject to Leap’s withdrawal policy, including the required 15 days’ written notice.
