Our Story
LEAP Gymnastics began with two friends, two daughters, and a shared love for a sport that had stayed with them since childhood.
Long before LEAP had a name, gymnastics was part of our own early lives. It was the place where we learned to be brave, to try again, to trust our bodies, and to feel proud of small improvements. Some days were about learning a new skill. Other days were about falling, getting back up, and discovering that progress does not always happen all at once.
Years later, we found ourselves watching our daughters grow into the same kind of children we once were: active, curious, determined, and happiest when they were moving. Our daughters became friends too, and gymnastics became part of their childhood in a new way.
As parents, we started to imagine a space where children could experience gymnastics with the same sense of excitement and confidence we remembered, but in a setting designed for today’s families. We wanted a club that felt organized, welcoming, and thoughtful from the moment families walked in. A place where children could build real gymnastics foundations, learn through proper progressions, and be supported by coaches who understand that every child develops at their own pace.
That idea slowly became LEAP.
We built LEAP Gymnastics in Markham as a place for children from Markham, Stouffville, and nearby communities to move, learn, and grow. Our programs are designed for different ages, abilities, and stages of development, from first classes and recreational gymnastics to advanced recreational training, competitive pathways, camps, summer programs, birthday parties, and drop-in opportunities.
At LEAP, gymnastics is not only about skills. It is about strength, coordination, balance, flexibility, focus, confidence, and the joy of discovering what your body can do. It is about learning how to listen, try, practise, and keep going.
Our coaching approach is grounded in safe technique, proper progressions, athlete readiness, and age-appropriate instruction. We want every child to feel supported, challenged, and proud of their progress, whether they are stepping into the gym for the first time or working toward a more focused training pathway.
LEAP was created with care because this sport meant something to us as children, and now it means something to our daughters.
We are proud to share that experience with families across Markham and York Region, and to welcome each child into a space where they can take their next leap with confidence.
