Before You Book
How are campers grouped?
Activities are planned with age, readiness and safety in mind. Coaches group and supervise campers throughout the day, with a 1:8 coach-to-participant ratio unless otherwise stated. Contact Leap if you would like to discuss your child's experience or readiness.
What should we pack?
Bring a labelled water bottle, lunch and two snacks. Wear comfortable athletic clothing, tie long hair back and remove jewelry. Leap does not provide food.
What are the participation requirements?
Campers must be fully potty-trained, comfortable participating independently and able to spend the day without a parent or guardian. Children should be able to follow coach instructions, stay with their group and participate without one-on-one support.
What additional fees apply?
The applicable annual Gymnastics Ontario membership fee is shown with the camp prices above and at checkout. There are no separate before-care or after-care fees. Camps are offered as full-day bookings only.
What happens if we cancel or miss a day?
There are no make-ups, refunds or credits for missed camp days. To request cancellation before the camp date or week begins, email Leap. Requests may be reviewed based on timing and camp availability. If approved, refunds exclude non-refundable fees, including the Gymnastics Ontario fee where applicable.
