Recreational Gymnastics
Leap’s recreational classes provide structured weekly gymnastics instruction from September to June. Coaches adapt progressions to each gymnast’s age, current ability and readiness, allowing children to join during the year where space is available.
Enrolment is month-to-month, with no full-year commitment. Fees and payment options are shown for your selected class. Late enrolment is prorated.
Families may withdraw with 15 days’ written notice. Please review our Cancellation and Refund Policy for complete billing, withdrawal and refund information.
View Enrolment and Withdrawal Policy
The Tuesday 45-minute Parent & Tot class is $90 for a full month. Fees are reduced when fewer sessions are scheduled in a month, and late registrations are prorated for the remaining sessions.
The dates shown for each class are the period during which that class operates.
New classes pause December 21–January 3 and March 15–21, and do not run on Ontario public holidays. Their applicable additional closures are February 15, March 26 and May 24, 2027.

Birthday Parties
Birthday party bookings
October 2026 - June 2027
For bookings made on or after October 6, 2026, Saturday and Sunday parties start at 1:30 p.m. Finish times depend on your package. If you have already booked, use the price, package inclusions and party time in your confirmation.
Additional party slot:
Saturday, November 21, 2026 at 3:30 p.m. Basic finishes at 5:15 p.m., Premium at 5:30 p.m. and Deluxe at 5:45 p.m.
Deposit & Payment:
Please book at least seven days in advance and choose your date and package at checkout. A $100 deposit plus HST ($113) is required to reserve your party and is applied toward the total cost.
Complete your party-details form at least seven days before your party. Your remaining balance will be shown before you submit. Once you submit the form, an itemized invoice will automatically be emailed to you, including HST and selected add-ons, with your deposit deducted.
Payment must be received by e-transfer to
info@leapgymnastics.ca
no later than the day before your party. Please include your invoice number in the transfer message. A receipt will be emailed once payment is confirmed. If your invoice remains unpaid after the due date, your party booking may be cancelled.
Your party email includes your party form and a guest waiver link. Please complete your party form at least seven days before your party. You can return to it to update your guest list and see which waivers we’ve received. Each participating child needs a waiver completed by their own parent or legal guardian, including the birthday child.
For bookings made on or after October 6, 2026: Basic $399 (10 children, 1 hour 45 minutes), Premium $589 (16 children, 2 hours), or Deluxe $849 (22 children, 2 hours 15 minutes), plus HST. Counts include the birthday child.
