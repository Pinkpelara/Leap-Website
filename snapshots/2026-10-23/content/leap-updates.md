Recreational Gymnastics
Recreational Gymnastics Registration Is Open
Registration is open for Leap’s weekly recreational gymnastics programs. Classes operate from September 2026 to June 2027, and children may join an available class at any point during the year. Coaches teach progressions according to each gymnast’s age, ability and readiness.
Programs include Leap Together Parent & Tot, Little Leapers for age three, Ready Set Leap for ages four to five, Rising Stars for ages six to seven, Flight Crew for ages eight and older, Boys Adventure Gym, Floor Tumbling & Obstacle Skills, and Leap Plus advanced recreational classes.
Enrolment is month-to-month, with no full-year commitment. Tuition is paid monthly while enrolled, and withdrawal requires 15 days’ written notice.
Weekday, evening and weekend options are available.

Friday Try Leap Drop-In Gymnastics
New to Leap Gymnastics? Start with a drop-in session before choosing a regular weekly class.
Try Leap drop-ins are structured, coach-led recreational gymnastics classes for new families who would like to experience Leap before registering for a regular weekly program.
Ages 3–5:
Fridays 4:00–5:00 p.m.
Ages 6–12:
Fridays 6:30–7:30 p.m.
Dates:
September 11, 2026–June 18, 2027 · 38 available Fridays
Cost:
$25 per registered child per selected date
Families may book more than one available drop-in session while deciding whether a regular Leap program is the right fit for their child.
If a child attends Leap Gymnastics for more than three visits during the Gymnastics Ontario membership year, including through drop-ins, camps, classes, or other Leap activities, Leap will contact the family and charge the $50 annual Gymnastics Ontario participant registration fee.
