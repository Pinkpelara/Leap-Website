Party favours and drinks

Your Deluxe package includes one Mini Party Favour Bag for each participating child, including the birthday child. Each bag contains two or three small party favours; contents vary.

The Cold Drink Station is also included, with juice, soft drinks and bottled water for children and adults. The selection depends on availability.

Food and cake

Please bring food ready to serve, such as pizza, sandwiches, fruit, packaged snacks, cake or cupcakes. We can’t accommodate ice cream, hot catered meals, buffets, heavily sauced food or anything that needs reheating or warming equipment.

Decorations

Your package includes a full-height half balloon garland and birthday plates, cups, napkins and forks chosen by Leap. We do not offer custom themes.

Will you bring any decorations?

You’re welcome to bring decorations that fit on the tables or stand on their own. Please don’t attach anything to the walls or fixtures. Your family is responsible for putting out and taking home any decorations you bring. We can’t add tables, move furniture or set up decorations brought from home.

    Party-day information

    Arrival and departure

    Your family may arrive at 1:20 p.m., up to 10 minutes before your party starts, to drop off food, cake and party items. Please ask guests to arrive at 1:30 p.m.

    Your party finishes at 3:45 p.m.. Allow time for packing up and saying goodbye so everyone is ready to leave by then. Leap handles the included cleanup.

    A late-departure charge of $40 + HST applies for each additional 30 minutes or part of 30 minutes. This charge does not extend your booking.

    Parents and guests

    A parent or guardian of the birthday child must stay throughout the party. Other parents may drop their children off or stay in the viewing or celebration area. Seating for accompanying adults is limited, so please let your guests know.

    Only participating children and Leap staff are allowed on the gym floor or equipment. Parents and guests must wear socks in the viewing area and party room.

    Payment

    Your $113 deposit has been paid and will go toward the total cost. The remaining balance, including HST, additional children and any extras, is payable during the party by e-transfer to info@leapgymnastics.ca.
