## 2. Market research findings

### School hours (why 4:00 PM is failing)
YRDSB/YCDSB elementary dismissal times vary by school, mostly between ~3:10 and ~3:50 PM. After pickup, parking, and driving across Markham, a 4:00 start is only reachable for families within minutes of Anderson Ave. **Richmond Hill Gymnastics Club — the 40-year incumbent — starts its earliest weekday class at 4:35 PM.** Your 12.5% fill at 4:00 vs 34% at 5:15 confirms the market has already voted.

### Competitor benchmark (Richmond Hill Gymnastics Club)
- Weekday classes: Mon/Wed 4:35, 5:45, 6:50 PM. Weekend: Saturday morning is their biggest day — six time slots, 9:00 AM–1:40 PM.
- Pricing: 55-min class $420/12 weeks ≈ **$35/class**. Your $950/37-38 classes ≈ **$25.70/class** — you are ~26% cheaper per class. That's a marketable advantage you aren't advertising.
- They sell 12-week sessions (lower commitment); you sell a full season. Your 10 monthly instalments soften this — lead with "$95/month, join anytime, prorated."
- TriStar (Markham) and ASF (Richmond Hill) are the other nearby players; TriStar publishes no pricing and gates via phone/email — your transparent online registration is a genuine edge.

### Demographics (Markham)
- ~53,880 children aged 0–14 (2021 census); median household income ~$104,000.
- Strong academic/enrichment culture — weekday evenings compete with tutoring; **weekend mornings are the premium family-activity window**, which matches your data (weekends 36% vs weekdays 22%).
- Large Chinese and South Asian communities: multilingual promo (WeChat/RedNote, Mandarin/Cantonese/Tamil) is a real lever competitors underuse.

### Industry trends
- **Ninja/obstacle is the fastest-growing kids' movement format**, with clubs reporting 50/50 boy/girl splits. Your only full weekday class is the obstacle-style one (Wed Floor Tumbling & Obstacle Skills 8/8) and Boys Adventure is your best Monday class — the trend shows up in your own data.
- Gymnastics Ontario: ~200 member clubs, 85,000–125,000+ participants; recreational demand concentrates in ages 3–7 (your Ready Set Leap results match).
- Daytime parent-tot/preschool/homeschool programming is a growing revenue stream for gyms whose floors sit empty 9 AM–4 PM on weekdays.

## 3. Recommendations (in priority order)

### A. Restructure the weekday grid: two blocks, not three *(biggest single fix)*
Kill the 4:00 PM block and run **4:45–5:45 and 6:00–7:00** (or keep 5:15/6:30 and simply drop 4:00). Effects:
- 16 mostly-empty classes disappear; coaches concentrate into blocks parents can actually reach.
- The 13 registered kids at 4:00 (Thu has 9 of them) move 45–75 min later — **needs your approval + parent contact before any change.**
- Thursday 4:00 is the exception (9/32) — if its families can't shift, keep Thursday as the only early block.

### B. Build Friday after school — it's an empty day you're paying rent on
Friday currently has one 9 AM class (1 kid) out of 78. Add a proven-winner lineup: 4:45/5:15 — Ready Set Leap, Rising Stars, Little Leapers; 6:00/6:30 — Ninja/Obstacle co-ed, Flight Crew. Friday evening has no tutoring conflict and no competitor lock-in.

### C. Add sections where you have waitlist pressure
- **Sunday Ready Set Leap: both sections full.** Your slot rules allow a 5th class per block (40-kid cap) — add a 5th class at Sun 9:00 and/or 10:15. This is immediate revenue at zero marketing cost.
- **Wed 6:30 Floor Tumbling & Obstacle full** — add a second section Thu or Fri 6:30, and market it as "Ninja/Obstacle" to ride the format trend.

### D. Rescue Leap Plus (9 kids across 8 sections, $142.50/mo)
Consolidate to **3 sections max** (Sat AM, Sun AM, one weekday 6:30) and reposition as **assessment-based/invite-only advanced stream** — scarcity sells at the premium price. Moving 9 kids requires approval. Cutting the 5 near-empty sections frees ~7.5 coach-hours/week.

### E. Fix the weekend concurrency conflict *(flag)*
Sat 10:15–11:00: four block classes (40-kid cap) **plus** two overlapping 9:30–11:00 Leap Plus classes = 6 concurrent classes / 56 potential kids — over your 5-class/40-kid limit at full fill. Same risk Sun 9:30. If Leap Plus consolidates (D), schedule the survivors 11:30–1:00 where the floor is emptiest.

### F. Relaunch Saturday 11:30 (currently 3/32)
Replace two zero-reg classes with a co-ed **Ninja/Obstacle** block and a second Ready Set Leap section (your two proven formats), positioned as "sleep in, still make gymnastics."

### G. Fill weekday daytimes with new revenue (no conflict with after-school rule)
The gym is empty 9 AM–4 PM Mon–Thu. Add: Parent & Tot + preschool morning classes (Tue/Wed/Thu 9:30–11:30), a weekly **homeschool block**, and keep growing PA-day camps. Markham's multigenerational households (grandparent/nanny caregivers) are the target. Low risk — start with 2–3 classes.

### H. Marketing sprint for the next 4 weeks (27% with season starting Sept 8)
1. **Price the comparison:** "From $25.70/class — the best value in York Region" (vs ~$35 nearby).
2. **Free trial class** — TriStar offers one; you should match and out-convert with online booking.
3. **Camp-to-class conversion:** every summer-camp family gets a personal "reserve your fall spot" email + the sibling 5% reminder.
4. **School-zone targeting:** flyers/partnerships at elementaries within 5 km; after-school demand is hyper-local.
5. **Multilingual social + geo-targeted Meta/Instagram ads** within 7 km of Anderson Ave, weekend-morning creative first (your highest-converting product).
6. **Scarcity messaging:** "Sunday Ready Set Leap SOLD OUT — 3 spots left Saturday" — real urgency, and it's true.

### I. What NOT to do
- Don't cut Thursday 4:00 without talking to its 9 families.
- Don't discount tuition — you're already the price leader; discounting erodes $95/mo positioning. Use the existing Early-Bird-style perks instead.
- Don't add more Little Leapers weekday sections until the 4:00→4:45 move proves out (age-3 parents are the most time-sensitive).
